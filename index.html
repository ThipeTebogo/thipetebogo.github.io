About Me

Hello, my name is Tebogo Nkadimeng, a Data Analyst with hands-on experience in Microsoft Excel, SQL, Power BI, Power Query, and data modelling, supported by practical business intelligence projects and continuous self-directed learning.

I enjoy working with data to uncover patterns, generate meaningful insights, and turn complex information into clear, practical solutions that support better business decisions. My experience also includes building interactive dashboards, transforming and modelling data, and developing reporting solutions with a strong focus on accuracy, usability, and business value.

As technology continues to evolve, I'm expanding my capabilities in Artificial Intelligence and Generative AI, using tools such as ChatGPT and Claude to support data analysis, research, problem-solving, productivity, and learning. I'm interested in exploring how analytics, business intelligence, and AI can work together to solve real-world business problems.

I'm a continuous learner with a strong analytical and problem-solving mindset, always looking for opportunities to strengthen my technical skills, work with meaningful data, and create solutions that make information easier to understand and act upon.
